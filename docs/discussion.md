# Discussion 
During this project, we encountered significant challenges in maintaining objectivity while reviewing the top 50 comments. Our backgrounds and beliefs inevitably influenced our perceptions of offensiveness, making it difficult to evaluate both the LLM’s outputs and the Perspective API scores without bias.

In answering our second research question, we wanted to explore what prompts lead to toxicity and why the LLMs generated certain outputs when prompted. Unfortunately, we did not have sufficient computational resources to delve into more advanced methods of understanding these underlying mechanisms. This remains an interesting avenue for future research, where better technical infrastructure and refined analytical approaches could provide deeper insights.

Overall, we learned a great deal throughout this project. However, when creating our project plan, we underestimated both our unfamiliarity with certain tasks and the time they would require. This included setting up LLMs, scoring their outputs, working with APIs, learning to work with Captum, and thoroughly reviewing code to grasp its functionality. Limited computational power further extended waiting times for generating results. These factors ultimately prevented us from accomplishing all the goals outlined in our project plan—most notably, addressing our first research question and creating a handbook.
